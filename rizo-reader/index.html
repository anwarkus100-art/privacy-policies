Rizo Reader – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

App: Rizo Reader (com.rizo.rizo_reader) for Android

Developer: A&R Solution BD. · Contact: anwar.kus@gmail.com

Rizo Reader is a free app for reading PDF, EPUB, Word (DOCX), TXT, FB2 and HTML files, listening to them with text-to-speech, extracting text from scanned pages (OCR) and making PDFs from photos. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. Summary

  • No account, no sign-in and no ads.
  • We (A&R Solution BD.) do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.
  • Your books, documents and photos are processed on your phone and are not uploaded by the app.

2. Files you open

  • When you open or import a file, the app saves a private copy inside its own storage on your phone so the book stays in your library. Other apps cannot read this storage.
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, collections and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • Text extraction (OCR) for scanned PDFs runs fully on your phone (Tesseract engine inside the app).
  • App data is not included in device backups (backup is turned off), so it is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Camera, photos and document scanning

  • Scan PDF and the camera option in Create new PDF use the Google ML Kit document scanner, which runs inside Google Play services. The app itself does not ask for camera permission. The scanned pages are given to the app and saved on your phone.
  • Create new PDF → Gallery opens your phone's photo picker. Only the photos you pick are read, made smaller and turned into a PDF on your phone.
  • Google Play services may send limited diagnostic and usage information about the ML Kit scanner to Google (for example app and device information, and performance data) to keep the service working and improve it. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Read aloud (text-to-speech)

  • Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example "Speech Services by Google" or the Samsung engine). Most voices work offline. If you choose a voice that needs the internet, that engine's provider may process the text to create speech, under its own privacy policy. Rizo Reader does not send your text anywhere itself.

5. Notifications and background work

  • The app shows a notification while it reads aloud or extracts text in the background, so you can control or stop it. The app asks for notification permission on Android 13 and newer. No notification content is sent off the phone.

6. Email

  • "Send feedback" opens your own email app. We only receive what you choose to send us, and we use it only to reply to you.

7. Children

  • Rizo Reader is a general reading tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Security

  • The app accepts files only from safe places, works without an account and does not upload your files. Communication by Google Play services is encrypted by Google.

9. Your choices

  • You can delete any book in the app (Move to Trash → Delete forever), clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

10. Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same link with a new effective date.
